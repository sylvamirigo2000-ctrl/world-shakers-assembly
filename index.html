Our Vision

The vision of World Shakers Assembly

01

To Raise Great People (Giants) Who Carry Power, Influence, and Impact in Our Generation, With a Divine Touch

Ephesians 4:13 · Joshua 14:12

To raise a generation of people who are spiritually mature, powerful, influential, and able to produce lasting results while carrying the presence and purpose of God.

02

To Raise a Generation That Burns With the Fire of the Holy Spirit

Acts 2 · Romans 12:11 · Joel 2:28

To build a generation that is passionate for God, empowered by the Holy Spirit, and committed to advancing His kingdom with zeal and spiritual fire.

03

To Raise People Who Shake Dark Systems and Bring Divine Transformation

Acts 17:26

To develop kingdom influencers who challenge ungodly systems, bring positive change, and establish the purposes of God in every sphere of society.

04

To Raise Royal and Priestly Families That Reign in Life Through the Grace of Christ

Revelation 1:6 · Romans 5:17

To establish families that understand their identity in Christ, walk in authority, and represent God's kingdom through righteous leadership and influence.

05

To Build a Network of Giants Connected for the Advancement of God's Kingdom

Ecclesiastes 4:9 · Ephesians 4:16

To create a united community of kingdom-minded people who work together, support one another, and expand the influence of God's kingdom across nations.

06

To Raise Financially Blessed People Who Use Their Wealth to Advance God's Kingdom and Become a Blessing to Nations

Deuteronomy 8:18 · 3 John 1:2 · Proverbs 10:22 · 2 Corinthians 9:8 · Psalm 112:1-3

To raise kingdom financiers who understand that wealth is a tool from God to serve His purposes, impact communities, and bless generations.

07

To Raise Awakened Giants Who Fulfill Their Divine Purpose

Ephesians 5:14 · Isaiah 60:1

To awaken people to their calling, identity, and destiny so they can rise and fulfill God's assignment for their lives.

08

To Build a Fearless Family of Believers Who Stand Firm in Faith Against Everything That Opposes God's Purpose

2 Timothy 1:7 · Joshua 1:8-9

To develop a strong family of believers who walk in courage, faith, and obedience while standing victorious against every challenge.

09

To Establish a Global Movement of Kingdom Champions Who Carry God's Presence and Influence

To create a worldwide movement of transformed individuals who represent Christ and impact communities, nations, and generations.

10

To Prepare a Generation That Will Reveal the Glory of God on Earth

To raise people who demonstrate God's power, love, wisdom, and kingdom values in every area of life.
